## 🧠 Yang Dipelajari · *What I Learned*

- **Perkenalan peserta.** Sesi dibuka dengan perkenalan dari masing-masing peserta.<br>
  *Participant introductions. The session opened with each participant introducing themselves.*
- **Mengenal Digital Skola.** *Class representative* (CR) menjelaskan jadwal kelas, memperkenalkan mentor, dan memandu penggunaan LMS Digital Skola: cara presensi serta mengerjakan quiz, *homework*, dan proyek.<br>
  *Getting to know Digital Skola. The class representative (CR) walked through the class schedule, introduced the mentors, and showed how to use the Digital Skola LMS: taking attendance and completing quizzes, homework, and projects.*

- **Pembentukan kelompok.** Peserta dibagi ke beberapa kelompok, lalu setiap kelompok memilih nama dan mempresentasikan filosofinya.<br>
  *Team formation. Participants were split into groups, and each group chose a name and presented the philosophy behind it.*
